What is QUIKO?

QUIKO is a personal habit and progress planner designed to help people organize their daily routines, track habits, monitor step goals and reflect on their progress.

Our goal is to make everyday consistency easier through a simple and accessible digital experience.

Meet the Creator

QUIKO was created by Abiram Naik Kethavathu, an independent developer interested in building useful digital products and technology solutions.

The project is developed under ARN Digital, a web development and digital solutions brand.
